The story

Two retail guys who started an agency at night.

$1b+Brett's store portfolio at Myer, NSW and ACT
$60m+Rowayne's stores across NSW
7 yrssince our first dental client

We started GYA in 2015. Back then Brett and I both worked at Myer during the day and built the agency at night. Brett was General Manager looking after NSW and ACT, a store portfolio worth over a billion dollars. I was a store manager running multiple stores across NSW, a bit over 60 million between them.

We took what we learnt inside one of Australia's biggest retailers, the targets, the accountability, the "why is this number down and what are we doing about it by Thursday" conversations, and used it to run a digital marketing company. That conversation is the one most agencies have quietly designed themselves out of. We kept it.

We never set out to work in a niche. Before the first dentist there were cafes, retailers, tradies, gyms and professional services. Then about seven years ago we took on a dental practice, The Caringbah Dentists, run by Dr Ram. It went really well. Well enough that the practice expanded, people noticed, and the referrals kept coming.

Today we manage over 100 dental clinics and around 30 other industries. Dental is what we're known for. It's not all we do.

What we don't do

  • We don't take on two dental clinics within 7km of each other, so we're never bidding against ourselves with your money.
  • We don't give you excuses.
  • We don't beat around the bush.
  • We don't set unrealistic expectations, and we won't promise you page one by Friday.
  • We don't take you on as a client if we think you're a bad fit.
  • And we don't spend your money marketing ourselves. What you pay us goes towards your marketing, not ours.

What we do do

  • Work hard. Work our team hard, and keep them accountable.
  • Find the problem, then fix it.
  • Fill your books the ways we know how to: Google Ads, lead generation through a platform we built ourselves called SmileOX, and local SEO (the map listing), because nobody scrolls down to the organic results anymore.
  • Get AI noticing your site, so when someone asks ChatGPT or Google's AI for a business like yours, your chances beat the one down the road.
  • Talk to you every fortnight, so you always know where things are at.

We genuinely get anxiety when your books are empty. So we go and fix it.

The formula

Marketing isn't rocket science.

Everyone says they do it differently. Even Elon Musk, with actual rocket science, fails time and time again. So we keep it simple and do it properly.

  1. Build a solid foundation

    Long term SEO with local SEO at the core, and future-proof your brand for AI search. This is the part that keeps working when you pause the ads.

  2. Build a solid Google Ads strategy, then talk about it

    We catch up with you every fortnight to see if it's working. We still send reports, but we don't hide behind them. You'll always know what we're doing.

  3. Build a lead generation machine

    SmileOX nurtures and triages every lead, then your team takes over with people who are ready to buy.

There are two ways someone finds you online. They search, or they see you. Fix those two and you're halfway there. The rest is a funnel that makes sure nothing slips through the cracks.
