In every build

The boring stuff, done properly.

The prettiest dental site in Australia is worthless if it scores 40 on mobile and nobody can work out how to book.

  • Speed first

    Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5s, no layout shift, video and images compressed properly. Google ranks on this stuff so we tune for it.

  • Mobile first

    Most dental traffic is on a phone. We design the phone version first and scale it up, not the other way round.

  • Video banners

    Clinic walk-throughs and team footage, compressed and lazy-loaded so they look cinematic without costing you speed.

  • Booking and SmileOX

    Online booking plugged in, and every form routed into SmileOX for instant reply, nurture and triage.

  • Built to rank

    Treatment pages, suburb pages, FAQ and LocalBusiness schema, clean URLs, internal linking. Ready from day one.

  • Accessible and compliant

    Proper contrast and keyboard navigation, AHPRA-aware content, privacy handled for Australian clinics.
