Some of what we've built.

A look at recent websites, branding and video. Each one below says what it was built to do, and why it was built that way.

Footscray Dental Studio

Brand · Website · Google Ads

An established practice on Barkly St, taken over by new owners and renamed. That's a tricky one. Half the job is winning new patients, and the other half is making sure the old ones don't assume the place shut down.

So the old name is mentioned plainly, the new patient offer sits on the homepage with a price on it, and every treatment they do has its own page, from check-ups through to All-on-X. The Google Ads went live pointing at those same pages, so the brand, the site and the ads all turned up together.

Website · Positioning · Suburb pages

This practice knows exactly what it is: whole-body, low-tox dentistry. That's a great position and an easy one to overdo, because the same site also has to work for a parent booking two kids in for a clean.

The hero leads with the idea, the exam packages sit right under it with prices on them, and each suburb they draw patients from gets its own page. Someone who's never heard of holistic dentistry gets it straight away, and someone who went looking for it knows they've found the right place.

Website · Local SEO

What they share

Different businesses. Same spine.

These are six different personalities and six different budgets, but the bones underneath are the same.

A page for every service, because a service in a list can't rank and a page about it can. An enquiry route on every screen. Video and images shrunk down and loaded a moment after the rest of the page, so it looks good without making anyone wait. And a build light enough to hold up on a phone with two bars of reception, which is how a lot of people will meet the business for the first time.

None of it is the sort of thing you notice at a glance. All of it is the reason these sites keep working long after launch.
