Open ChatGPT and ask it for the best business in your suburb doing what you do. Someone gets named. If it isn't you, that's a decision being made about your business, in a room you're not in.

This is already happening. Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity answer questions like "who does dental implants near me", "best physio in Cronulla" or "electrician open today" with a short list and a recommendation.

## Why this one is easy to miss

When you slip off page one of Google, you can see it happen. Traffic drops, rankings move, somebody notices.

When an AI quietly stops naming you, nothing happens at all. No alert, no line on a report, no customer ringing to say they went with the business the assistant suggested. You just get fewer enquiries, and no explanation for them.

## What decides who gets named

The businesses being recommended aren't necessarily better than you. They're easier for a machine to be confident about. In our experience that comes down to a few things.

**Their details match everywhere.** Same name, same address, same phone number, same services, on the website, the Google listing and every directory. When the details disagree, the machine is less sure, and less sure means less likely to name you.

**Their site says plainly what they do and where.** Not "solutions for your smile". Actual services, actual suburbs, in plain words. Plus the code underneath that spells it out for machines, called structured data or schema.

**Their pages answer the questions people actually ask.** People don't type "dentist Caringbah" into an AI assistant. They ask whether they need a crown or a filling, what an implant costs, and whether anyone can see them today. Pages that answer those questions plainly are the ones that get cited back.

**They have real reviews.** A steady flow of genuine reviews, answered properly, tells both Google and the AI tools that the business is active and trusted.

Almost none of that is expensive. It's just done, or it isn't.

## What this means for the rest of your marketing

Two other things shift alongside AI search.

**Speed matters more.** When an assistant hands someone two or three names, they often contact more than one. Being quick to answer used to be a nice touch. Now it's often the whole difference between the enquiry being yours and being someone else's. That's a big part of why we built SmileOX, which answers every enquiry in under a minute, day or night.

**Making ads got easy, knowing which to run didn't.** Anyone can produce fifty versions of an ad in an afternoon now. Picking which suburb, which offer and which service to push this month, then switching off the ones that aren't working, is still the actual job.

## A simple check you can do today

1. Ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Mode for the best business doing what you do in your suburb. Note who gets named.
2. Search your own business name and check that your name, address, phone number and services match exactly across your website and Google listing.
3. Look at your website's service pages. Does each main service have its own page that answers the questions customers ask before they book?

If the answer to any of those isn't what you hoped, that's where to start.
