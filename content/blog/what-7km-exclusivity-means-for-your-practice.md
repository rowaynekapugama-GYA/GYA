It means we won't take on another dental clinic within 7km of yours. If you're our client, the practice down the road can't hire us to do its Google Ads, its local SEO or its Meta ads. So the people writing your ads, building your treatment pages and working on your Google listing are never doing the same job for the clinic you're competing with for the same patients, the same searches and the same three spots on the map.

That's the short answer. The longer one is why it matters more in dental than people expect.

## Local search is a fixed number of spots

When someone searches "dentist near me" or "Invisalign" plus their suburb, Google shows three practices on the map. Everyone else sits below. Those three spots don't grow because more practices want them.

Ads work the same way. When two practices bid on the same search in the same area, they push each other's price up. One of them wins the click, the other pays more for trying.

Now picture one agency running both of those practices. Whose treatment page gets written first? Whose listing gets the fresh photos and the extra attention this month? Which account gets the better keywords? Even with the best intentions, the agency is working against itself, and one of the two clients is paying for that.

We didn't want to be in that spot, so we made the rule simple. One dental client per 7km.

## What it protects for you

**Your ad budget.** We build Google Ads per treatment and per suburb, with negative keyword lists from day one. That work is only worth something if we aren't building the same campaign for the clinic next door and bidding against ourselves with your money.

**Your map ranking.** The work we do on your listing, your treatment pages and your reviews is aimed at those three map spots. We're not quietly doing the same for the practice that's trying to take them off you.

**Your ideas.** On our fortnightly calls you'll tell us what's working, which treatments you want to grow, what your front desk is hearing. That stays with your account. It doesn't turn up in a competitor's strategy a month later.

**Your enquiries.** SmileOX, our own lead platform, answers new enquiries and texts back missed calls. The setup we build around your patients and your treatments is yours, not something we're tuning for the practice around the corner at the same time.

## Why 7km

Patients mostly book close to where they live or work. A practice a few suburbs over is chasing a lot of the same people you are. Once you get far enough apart, the overlap drops off and the conflict mostly goes away.

7km is the line we drew. It isn't perfect for every area, and we'd rather be a bit generous than leave a client wondering whether we're also helping the practice they lose patients to.

## What it costs us

Turning work down. If a practice we'd like to work with is within 7km of a clinic we already look after, the answer is no. We manage 100+ dental clinics across Australia, so the map fills up.

It also means we can't just grow by signing every practice in a busy area. We'd rather grow by doing good work and getting referred, which is how this started. Our first dental client was The Caringbah Dentists, about seven years ago, and the referrals grew from there.

## Questions to ask any agency

You don't need to work with us to use this. Whoever you're talking to, ask these before you sign:

1. **How many dental practices within a few kilometres of me do you already work for?** You want a straight number, not "we have strict processes".
2. **If a practice near me approaches you next month, will you take them on?** If the answer is yes, ask how they'd split their attention.
3. **Who owns the work if I leave?** Your Google Ads account, your website, your listing. These should be yours.
4. **Will the same person run my account and my competitor's?** Sometimes this is the real question underneath the first one.

None of these are trick questions. A good agency should be able to answer all four on the first call without having to check.

## What it doesn't mean

It isn't a guarantee you'll rank first. Nobody can promise that, and anyone who does is guessing. Map movement usually shows in six to ten weeks. Bigger treatment terms like "dental implants" take four to nine months. Google Ads can bring enquiries in within days.

What the rule does is make sure all the effort on your side of that 7km is going one way, toward booked patients for your practice.
