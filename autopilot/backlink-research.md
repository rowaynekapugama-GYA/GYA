# GYA backlink, listing and free-award opportunities

Researched 21 October 2026 for Generate Your Audience (generateyouraudience.com), Sydney dental marketing agency, founded 2015. Offices: Crows Nest and Parramatta NSW. Contact: hello@generateyouraudience.com, 1300 889 840.

## How this was verified, and the limits

- Every row below was checked by fetching a live page on 21 October 2026, unless the row says otherwise.
- The research environment could only fetch URLs that first appeared in a web search result. A handful of primary pages (True Local, Hotfrog AU, Localsearch, Clutch get-listed, Centaur, Agency Spotter home, Small Business Champion Awards site, webawards.com.au, mentionmatch.com) could not be opened directly. Where that happened, the row says "Verified via secondary page" and names the page actually read. Treat those rows as "very likely live, confirm the exact URL in a browser before you submit".
- Dofollow or nofollow is stated only where a page actually said so. Most directories do not say; assume nofollow unless you check the source code.
- "Contact via site form" means no email address was published on the page fetched.

Verification key: **V** = primary page fetched. **S** = verified via a secondary page (named in the row).
