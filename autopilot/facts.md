## How GYA works

- Will not take on two dental clinics within 7km of each other.
- Fortnightly calls with every client, not just reports.
- Does not spend client money marketing GYA.
- First week sequence: breakdown, strategy, local SEO and AI search foundations, Google Ads live, Meta creative, Meta ads live, SmileOX integrated.
- Google Ads built per treatment and per suburb, landing on treatment pages, with negative keyword lists from day one.
- Ads, landing pages and posts written to AHPRA advertising guidelines before they go live.

- Most clinics GYA manages put between 3% and 8% of revenue into marketing, mostly Google Ads and SEO.

## SmileOX

- GYA's own lead platform, not a reseller product. Spelled SmileOX.
- Answers new enquiries instantly from any source, asks qualifying questions, scores leads hot, warm or cool, nurtures the ones not ready (14-day sequences), texts back missed calls, and hands over to a human when needed.
- Site claim: first response to a new lead in under 60 seconds.
- Also used for agency clients beyond dental (labs, course providers, suppliers).

## Voice and copy rules

- Australian English. No em dashes anywhere, ever.
- Casual, human, first person where it fits. Must not read as AI-written.
- Anti-pitch stance: "We're not going to tell you why you should hire us."
- Measure success in booked patients, not impressions or clicks.
- Never disparage general dentists or draw negative comparisons against them. Position specialists on their own credentials.
- AHPRA: no testimonials about clinical care, no claims creating unreasonable expectations, no offers without clear terms, no restricted specialist titles for non-specialists. (These rules apply to client advertising. GYA's own reviews from practice owners about GYA's work are fine.)
