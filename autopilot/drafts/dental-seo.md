## The order we do it in

### 1. Your map listing first

Your Google Business Profile is the most valuable thing you own online, and most practices treat it as a form they filled in once.

We set the right primary category, list every treatment as a service, add real photos of your team and rooms, and make your hours accurate. Google leans towards practices that are open when someone searches, so hours matter more than they used to.

Then we make your name, address and phone number match everywhere they appear online. Mismatched details are the most common reason a good practice sits fourth in its own suburb.

### 2. Reviews, steadily

Reviews are one of the biggest things Google weighs on the map. A steady flow beats a burst. We set up a simple way for patients to leave one, and help you reply, without crossing AHPRA's line: patients can review you, but reviews can't be reused as testimonials in your ads.

### 3. A page for every treatment

"Dental implants" and "check-up and clean" are different searches from different people. Each treatment gets its own page that answers what patients actually ask: what it involves, roughly how long it takes, and how to book. A single services page rarely ranks first for anything.

### 4. The technical work underneath

Fast on a phone, clean structure, the right schema so Google and AI assistants know exactly what you do and where. It's the unglamorous part, and it's what holds everything else up.

### 5. AI search

Patients now ask ChatGPT and Google's AI whether they need a crown or a filling, or who can see them today. We write your pages so the answer is clear in the first lines, which is what those assistants quote. [Read more about AI search for dentists.]
