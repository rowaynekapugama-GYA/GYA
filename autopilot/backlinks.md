# GYA backlink outreach

The queue and the rules for earning links, listings, mentions and awards for generateyouraudience.com. The Thursday "Backlink outreach" scheduled task reads this file, works the next items, and emails Rowayne. Rowayne can add, remove or reorder anything by editing this file or replying to the Thursday email.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

## How the loop runs

1. **Thursday 6:45am Sydney.** The task:
   - reads Rowayne's reply to last week's batch email (searches Gmail for the thread) and sends the approved emails or submits the approved forms;
   - checks every outreach thread for replies and records them in the tracker below;
   - picks the next 10 targets from the queue, researches each one (recent article, the right person's name, what they'd want), writes the email, and sends Rowayne one batch email with all 10 drafts numbered;
   - adds any new targets it found during research to the queue.
2. **Rowayne replies** "send all", "send 1, 3, 5", "skip 4", or edits a draft inline. No reply means nothing is sent.
3. **After four weeks**, if Rowayne agrees, categories marked `auto` below are sent without waiting. Cold emails to named people stay on approval.

## Rules for every email

- From: the Gmail account connected to Claude. Sign as Rowayne, Founder, Generate Your Audience. Phone 1300 889 840.
- Facts only from facts.md. No client names or numbers that aren't cleared there.
- One ask per email. Short: under 150 words. No attachments on a first email.
- Open with something specific to them (an article they published, a feature they launched), not with GYA.
- Offer something before asking: a contributed article, a quote, a case study, data from 100+ clinics. A bare "please link to us" gets deleted.
- Australian English. No em dashes. Must read as a person, not a template.
- Last line, every time, for the Spam Act: "Generate Your Audience Pty Ltd, Crows Nest NSW. If you'd rather not hear from us again, reply with 'no thanks' and that's the end of it."
- One follow-up only, 10 days later, if there's no reply. Then the target is closed.
- Never more than 15 outbound emails in a week from this programme.
- Never email a dental practice. Practices are clients, not link targets.
