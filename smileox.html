Why we built it

Marketing stops at the enquiry. SmileOX doesn't.

We manage ads for over 100 clinics. The pattern was always the same. Good campaigns, real leads, then silence. An unanswered form, a voicemail nobody returned, a "we'll call you back" that never happened. The patient booked somewhere else.

So we built the thing that closes the gap. SmileOX sits between your marketing and your front desk, and it never takes a lunch break.

  • For clinics

    Enquiries answered, nurtured and booked. Dormant patients reactivated. Your front desk works a ranked list instead of a voicemail backlog.

  • For labs, suppliers and educators

    Clinic and dealer enquiries sorted by account size, course enquiries nurtured to enrolment. Same engine, pointed at B2B.

  • For your reporting

    Because SmileOX sees the lead and the booking, we can finally tell you cost per booked patient by channel and treatment. Not cost per click.

  • Included with GYA marketing

    SmileOX comes with every marketing engagement. It can also run on its own on top of your existing campaigns.
